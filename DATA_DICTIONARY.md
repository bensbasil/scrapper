# Data Dictionary

# Business Information

### business_name

Name of the business.

### category

Business category or industry.

### website

Official website URL.

### google_rating

Google Maps rating score.

### review_count

Number of reviews.

### phone

Business phone number.

### address

Business address.

### source_platform

Data source:

* Google Maps
* JustDial
* IndiaMart
* Website
* Registry

### scraped_at

Timestamp when record was collected.

---

# Website Analysis

### website_exists

Whether business has a website.

### ssl_enabled

Whether HTTPS is enabled.

### mobile_friendly

Whether website appears mobile optimized.

### meta_title_exists

Whether meta title exists.

### meta_description_exists

Whether meta description exists.

### contact_form_exists

Whether contact form exists.

### whatsapp_integration

Whether WhatsApp integration exists.

### social_links_count

Number of social links detected.

### website_load_score

Estimated website performance score.

# SEO Analysis

### seo_score
