# Project Context

## Project Name

Business Opportunity Intelligence Platform

---

## Goal

Build an AI-powered business intelligence and lead intelligence platform that identifies businesses likely needing:

* website development
* SEO
* automation
* dashboards
* AI integrations
* digital optimization services

The system should:

1. collect business data
2. enrich business information
3. analyze digital presence
4. detect pain points
5. generate opportunity scores
6. identify service opportunities
7. support outreach

---

## Core Purpose

Identify businesses where technical services can create measurable value.

This is not only a lead generation system.

It is:

Business Discovery → Intelligence → Opportunity Detection

---

## Current MVP Focus

Current MVP:

* business collection
* website analysis
* enrichment
* scoring logic
* business opportunity insights

collect
